# Identify your iPod before ordering parts

> **Do this first.** The same A1238 model number appears across several iPod generations. A listing that says only “iPod Classic A1238” is not enough to select storage, battery, screen, or rear-case parts.

## What to record

Photograph the front and back, then record the following in a build note:

- capacity engraved on the rear case;
- model number and the last three serial-number characters (where available);
- device capacity and software version from **Settings → About**, if it boots;
- rear-case depth and whether the original drive is thin or thick;
- existing hardware faults and every installed replacement part.

Use [Apple’s iPod identification page](https://support.apple.com/en-ca/103823) as the primary visual and model-number reference. It identifies the models below and is more reliable than a marketplace title.

## Common modding candidates

| Apple name | Introduced / original capacities | Practical identification cue | Ordering rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| iPod classic 160 GB (Late 2009) | September 2009 / 160 GB | Thin 160 GB Classic; Apple calls this the Late 2009 model | Use only 7th-gen-specific instructions after confirming the device. |
| iPod classic (120 GB) | September 2008 / 120 GB | 120 GB rear engraving | Do not substitute 7th-gen storage-limit claims. |
| iPod classic | September 2007 / 80 or 160 GB | 80 GB or 160 GB rear engraving | Confirm original capacity/firmware and physical drive before choosing storage parts. |
| iPod (5th generation, Late 2006) | September 2006 / 30 or 80 GB | Often called “5.5 gen” by sellers; Apple calls it 5th generation Late 2006 | Buy parts sold explicitly for the 5th-generation Video family. |
| iPod (5th generation) | October 2005 / 30 or 60 GB | Video-capable Click Wheel iPod; not a metal Classic | Its plastic front and case construction make it a different repair procedure. |
| iPod mini / iPod mini (2nd generation) | 2004 / 4 GB; 2005 / 4 or 6 GB | Small anodized-aluminum body; capacity and Click Wheel details distinguish versions | Use Mini-specific storage and battery guides. |

Apple’s page also shows other iPod families. This guide should never imply that iPod touch, nano, shuffle, and Classic parts interchange.

## A1238: the important warning

Apple lists **A1238** for the 2005 fifth-generation iPod, 2006 Late-2006 fifth-generation iPod, and the 2007/2008/2009 Classic family. Use the original capacity and the full Apple identification entry to distinguish them. Do not rely on the A-number by itself.

## Determine case depth without guessing

“Thin” and “thick” are fitment terms, not cosmetic descriptions. Confirm the original rear case/drive configuration and compare the replacement parts’ measured dimensions against a reputable compatibility reference. If the case has already been replaced, treat its appearance as untrusted until you inspect the internal configuration.

## Before adding a procedure

A model page should have an original labelled photograph showing:

1. front, rear engraving, ports, and hold switch;
2. the original capacity and model/serial markers;
3. thin versus thick case profile where that affects fit;
4. connectors and components that differ from nearby generations.

Do not republish Apple or retailer photography without permission. Link to Apple’s identification images, or create original comparison photography.
