# Guide index and reading order

This is the front door for the published guide. Each procedure should be reachable from here in no more than two clicks.

> **Prefer a visual starting point?** Visit [Watch & Learn](watch.html): a responsive thumbnail library of model-relevant videos and primary repair references.

> **Know the topic but not the page?** Use the lightweight [guide finder](find.html).

## 1. Plan your build

Before purchasing anything, record:

- exact iPod model and original capacity;
- case thickness (thin or thick) and condition;
- desired storage capacity and approximate library size;
- whether you want stock Apple firmware or Rockbox;
- every planned mod: storage, battery, enclosure, Bluetooth, USB-C, or other electronics.

Use [Apple’s identification guidance](https://support.apple.com/en-ca/103823) alongside the capacity/firmware information on the device. Later Classics look very similar; never order from appearance alone.

Read [Identify your iPod](General/identify-your-ipod.md) before choosing parts, then complete the [build fitment planner](General/fitment-planner.md) before ordering a battery or replacement rear case.

## 4. Restore, test, and close

Every hardware procedure ends with an **open-case test**. Do not close the case until storage is detected, the iPod restores, charging works, audio plays, and controls respond. Record what was installed and any observed issue; this makes future troubleshooting possible.

## Visual and link conventions

Readers need a visual at the point of risk, not a decorative gallery at the end.

- **Map:** one labelled overview image before disassembly, showing connectors and no-go zones.
- **Do:** an original or properly licensed close-up before each fragile connector, cable fold, or case-fit step. Add an arrow and short alt text.
- **Verify:** a photo or screenshot of the expected restore/diagnostic result.
- **Video:** place a clearly labelled `Watch before step X` link immediately above the step it supports; include creator, duration, model, and why it helps. Videos complement written steps and never replace a safety-critical instruction.
- **Licensing:** do not copy product photos, screenshots, or creator thumbnails without permission. Prefer your own photos, manufacturer media used with permission, iFixit images embedded through their guide, or openly licensed images with attribution.

## What needs verification before publication

- capacity and track-count limits by exact model/firmware;
- current adapter, battery, and enclosure fitment combinations;
- formatting and restore workflows on current macOS and Windows software;
- all external links and video references;
- every numerical runtime, charge-time, temperature, and cost claim.

Until these are bench-tested or directly sourced, describe them as compatibility reports—not guarantees.
