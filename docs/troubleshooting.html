Symptom-led support

Start with what you see.

Use symptoms to choose a safe first check. Do not treat a symptom as a diagnosis, and do not keep reopening the case without recording what changed.

Before anything else: back up data if the iPod mounts; stop if the battery is swollen, hot, torn, or smells unusual; change one variable at a time.
Storage / connection

Red X

With a flash conversion, first re-check adapter seating, ribbon alignment, and the exact media-preparation guidance. Do not assume a universal card-brand fix.

Storage / cable

Folder icon or sad iPod

Record the boot behaviour, inspect the drive cable/connection, and use iFixit’s symptom guidance before replacing parts.

Software / restore

Restore loop or failure

Keep the build open, document computer and operating-system details, and verify each storage variable before moving to software workarounds.

Battery

Won’t charge

Use a known-good cable and power source, inspect the connector, and stop immediately if the battery shows damage or swelling.

Fitment

Case will not close

Never force it. Re-open, confirm no cable is under a clip, and return to the fitment worksheet before trying again.

Controls / hardware

Controls or audio fail after opening

Inspect the relevant flex cable and latch under good light. Treat a changed symptom after reassembly as a connection issue until proven otherwise.

Make a useful troubleshooting record

  • Model, original capacity, and firmware
  • Exact new parts and their revisions
  • What changed immediately before the symptom
  • Photo of connector orientation and error screen
  • Computer, operating system, and restore result

Escalation boundary

If reseating a known-good connection does not change the symptom, do not repeatedly flex the same ribbon or attempt board-level repair without a model-specific plan. Preserve the evidence and seek a repair reference.
