Plain-language reference

Glossary

Short definitions for terms readers meet while planning or repairing an iPod. A definition tells you what a term means; the procedure tells you what to do.

↔

ZIF connector

A ribbon-cable socket with a moving latch. Release the latch before moving the ribbon; do not pull the cable to discover how it opens.

▣

Flash adapter

A board that lets the iPod’s original storage interface communicate with flash media instead of its mechanical drive.

◒

Thin / thick rear case

Physical enclosure depth. It affects drive, battery, and flex-cable clearance; it is not a cosmetic preference alone.

◎

Firmware

Software stored on the iPod that starts the device and controls its operation. Record its version when testing compatibility.

▤

Restore

The computer-driven process that reinstalls iPod software and prepares storage. It can erase the device, so back up first.

⌁

Rockbox

Alternative open-source firmware. Treat it as a separate software path with its own supported-device, installation, and recovery documentation.

⚡

Board-level modification

Work involving soldering or changes to the logic board. It needs a model-specific guide, appropriate equipment, and a recovery plan.

✓

Open-case test

Functional testing while the rear case is still unlatched. It is the safest point to discover a storage, cable, or fitment problem.

▧

Fitment

Whether all physical components fit and operate together without stress, pinched flex cables, gaps, or force during reassembly.
